# Databricks SQL Dashboard Guide

## Scope

The dashboard uses only existing business-ready Gold tables. The SQL is stored
in `src/dashboard/dashboard_queries.sql`; it does not repeat Silver validation
or Gold business logic.

Required visualizations:

1. Top 10 products by revenue
2. Customer revenue distribution
3. Customer segmentation

A weekly revenue trend is included as an optional fourth visualization.

## Setup

1. Open the Databricks SQL editor and select available SQL compute.
2. Copy one statement at a time from `dashboard_queries.sql`.
3. Run and save each required query.
4. Add the saved query results to a dashboard.
5. Configure each visualization using the mappings below.
6. Confirm displayed totals against the corresponding Gold table before
   sharing the dashboard.

## Visualization 1 — Top 10 Products by Revenue

Purpose:

Identify the products producing the highest qualifying completed-order revenue.

Gold source:

```text
ecommerce_sales.gold.sales_by_product
```

Visualization type:

```text
Bar chart
```

Field mappings:

- X/category: `product_name`
- Y/value: `total_revenue`
- Optional tooltip fields: `category`, `total_orders`

Filters and ordering:

- No interactive filter is required.
- The SQL orders by `total_revenue` descending and limits the result to 10
  products.

Business interpretation:

Longer bars identify the strongest revenue-generating products. `total_orders`
helps distinguish products driven by order volume from products driven by
higher order values.

## Visualization 2 — Customer Revenue Distribution

Purpose:

Show how qualifying completed-order revenue is distributed across customers,
including customers with zero qualifying revenue.

Gold source:

```text
ecommerce_sales.gold.revenue_by_customer
```

Visualization type:

```text
Histogram
```

Field mappings:

- Values/X measure: `total_revenue`
- Y measure: customer frequency/count generated by the histogram
- Optional detail fields: `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `customer_segment`

Filters:

- No filter is required.
- A dashboard user may optionally filter by `customer_segment`.

Business interpretation:

The histogram shows whether revenue is broadly distributed or concentrated
among a smaller number of customers. A large zero-value bin represents
customers with no qualifying completed orders.

## Visualization 3 — Customer Segmentation

Purpose:

Show the customer population assigned to High-Value, Repeat, One-Time, and
Inactive segments.

Gold source:

```text
ecommerce_sales.gold.customer_segmentation
```

Visualization type:

```text
Pie chart
```

Field mappings:

- Category: `segment_type`
- Value: `customer_count`
- Optional tooltip fields: `avg_revenue`, `total_revenue`

Filters:

- No filter is required.

Business interpretation:

The relative slice sizes show the composition of the customer base. Revenue
tooltips provide context on the economic contribution of each segment.

## Optional Visualization 4 — Weekly Revenue Trend

Purpose:

Show how qualifying completed-order revenue changes over time at a weekly
grain.

Gold source:

```text
ecommerce_sales.gold.daily_weekly_trends
```

Visualization type:

```text
Line chart
```

Field mappings:

- X axis: `period_start`
- Y axis: `total_revenue`
- Optional tooltip fields: `total_orders`, `avg_order_value`

Filters:

- The supplied query fixes `period_type = 'WEEKLY'`.
- Change the filter to `DAILY` if a daily view is preferred. Do not mix daily
  and weekly rows in one ungrouped series because they represent different
  grains.

Business interpretation:

The line highlights growth, decline, and unusual weekly revenue movements.
Order count and average order value help explain whether a change is driven by
volume or order size.

## Validation

Before considering the dashboard complete:

- confirm the product query returns at most 10 rows,
- confirm histogram values come directly from customer-level Gold revenue,
- confirm segment customer counts reconcile with
  `ecommerce_sales.gold.customer_segmentation`,
- confirm no query references Bronze or Silver tables,
- confirm the optional trend is clearly labeled as weekly.
